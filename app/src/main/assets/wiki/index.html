CRITICAL SECURITY ADVISORY & OPSEC PROTOCOLS MANDATORY

NO CLOUD BACKUPS • ZERO MASTER BACKDOORS • MATHEMATICAL FINALITY

CipherVault employs zero telemetry, connects to no remote servers, and preserves no backdoor key vaults. If you lose your Password or alter your Secret Pepper without a backup, your encrypted data is permanently, irrevocably lost. No technician, intelligence agency, or supercomputer cluster can restore it.

Follow these operational security (OpSec) commandments to guarantee airtight safety for your confidential records:

1

Write the Secret Pepper on Physical Paper or Metal Plate

Navigate to Settings > Secret Pepper. Write down the 32-character hex sequence on archival-grade paper or engrave it onto a stainless steel plate. Store this physical card in a fireproof home safe or safety deposit box.

2

NEVER Store Passwords or Pepper in Cloud Storage

Do NOT upload your Secret Pepper or passphrases to Google Keep, iCloud Notes, Telegram Saved Messages, WhatsApp, or email drafts. Cloud services are vulnerable to credential stuffing, insider threats, and subpoenas.

3

Keep Secret Pepper Synchronized Across Devices

If you encrypt a file on Device A and wish to decrypt it on Device B, both devices must have the identical 32-character Secret Pepper configured in Settings. Alternatively, use Standalone Mode if transferring data to third parties who do not have your Pepper.

4

Enable FLAG_SECURE & Biometric/PIN Enclave

Under Settings, keep Screenshot & Task Switcher Guard (FLAG_SECURE) active. This blocks Android OS screen scrapers, background malware, and preview thumbnails in Recent Apps. Set a Numeric PIN or Master Password to prevent physical unauthorized access.

Why Interception & Decryption is Mathematically Impossible CRYPTANALYSIS

THE POWER OF 256-BIT EXPONENTIATION

A 256-bit key space represents \(2^{256} \approx 1.1579 \times 10^{77}\) unique possible keys. To contextualize this magnitude: there are approximately \(10^{80}\) atoms in the entire observable universe.

Here is why an adversary capturing your encrypted message cannot read or reverse it:

A

Exhaustive Brute-Force Infeasibility

Suppose the most powerful supercomputing cluster on Earth could test 100 Trillion keys per second (\(10^{14}\) keys/sec). It would still require \(3.67 \times 10^{55}\) years to exhaustively search the key space. The universe itself is only \(1.38 \times 10^{10}\) years old.

B

600,000 PBKDF2 Rounds Paralyzes GPU Cracking

Standard hashing functions allow specialized GPU rigs to calculate billions of guesses per second. By tuning PBKDF2 to 600,000 HMAC-SHA256 computational rounds, each single guess demands massive CPU memory cycles, slowing GPU rigs to a crawl of fewer than 50 guesses per second.

C

Dual-Factor Isolation (Password + Pepper)

CipherVault binds your user password with the 32-character Secret Pepper. Even if an attacker places a keylogger on your keyboard and steals your master password, they cannot decrypt your messages without the 32-character Pepper stored inside your app instance.

D

AEAD Tamper Detection (Integrity Rejection)

Both AES-GCM and ChaCha20-Poly1305 utilize Authenticated Encryption with Associated Data (AEAD). If an attacker alters even a single bit in the encrypted payload, the Poly/GCM tag fails verification instantly, aborting before any decryption algorithm runs.

Complete Workflow Manual GUIDE

1. Text Encryption Modes

When encrypting sensitive text or messages, you have two strategic choices:

  • Standard Mode (Default): Combines your Password + 32-character Secret Pepper. Delivers maximum military-grade defense. Both devices must share the same Pepper.
  • Standalone Mode (Without Pepper): Encrypts with password only. Ideal for sharing files or texts with friends who do not possess your Secret Pepper. Requires a strict high-entropy password (min 14 chars with uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols).

2. Large File Streaming Vault (1GB+)

Unlike ordinary encryption apps that load an entire file into device RAM (causing crashes), CipherVault streams data in 128KB chunks:

  • Zero RAM Bottlenecks: Encrypt multi-gigabyte video recordings, disk backups, and PDFs without memory overflow.
  • Proprietary .cvault Container: Embeds original filename and extension securely in the authenticated header. Restores your exact file name upon decryption.
  • Stealth Custom Extensions: Choose .cenc, .enc, or type your custom extension for plausible deniability.

3. Project Draft Vault

Save notes, confidential memos, and sensitive codes in the Projects tab. Backed by an encrypted local Room SQLite database, projects allow multi-session drafting before one-click encryption and export.

Frequently Asked Security Questions FAQ
Can CipherVault developers decrypt my files if I forget my password?
Absolutely not. CipherVault is a pure client-side zero-knowledge security tool. There are no master recovery keys, no cloud servers, and no backdoor interfaces. If you lose your credentials, your data is mathematically unrecoverable.
Why do I get "Decryption failed or invalid key / pepper"?
Because CipherVault uses Authenticated Encryption (AEAD), even a 1-character typo in your password, an altered Secret Pepper, or a truncated/modified ciphertext string will cause the authentication tag verification to fail immediately. Double-check your password and verify your 32-character Secret Pepper in Settings.
Does CipherVault leave plain text in Android's clipboard or RAM?
CipherVault includes an automatic memory zeroization engine: after every cryptographic operation, all temporary byte arrays and key buffers are filled with zeros. Furthermore, whenever you copy text to your clipboard, a 30-second countdown timer automatically wipes the clipboard to prevent other apps from spying on it.
Is this quantum-resistant?
Symmetric algorithms with 256-bit key lengths (like AES-256 and ChaCha20) remain secure against Grover's algorithm on hypothetical quantum computers, which only reduces effective brute-force complexity from 256 bits to 128 bits. 128 bits of quantum security still requires \(3.4 \times 10^{38}\) operations—far beyond any conceivable quantum computing system.
